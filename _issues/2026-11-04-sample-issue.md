## Level Up: Word of the Week

### Hallucination

When an AI tool states something false as if it were true, like a made-up fact, quote, or source. It isn't lying on purpose. It predicts likely-sounding words, and sometimes those words are wrong. Always check facts that matter.

## Try This

### Turn your notes into a practice quiz

Use this before a test to find out what you don't know yet.

```text
Here are my class notes: [paste notes]. Ask me 5 questions about them, one at a time. Wait for my answer before asking the next one, then tell me what I got wrong and why.
```

**What to look for:** Check the AI's corrections against your notes or textbook. If it "corrects" something your notes got right, trust your notes.

## Is This OK? Academic Honesty

### "I wrote my essay myself, then asked AI to fix the grammar. Is that OK?"

**My answer:** It depends on your instructor, so check the syllabus or ask. Many allow grammar help but not rewriting. If you're not sure, tell your instructor what you did. Being upfront is never the wrong move.
